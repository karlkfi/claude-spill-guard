# Correct the `&&` errexit mechanism in CLAUDE.md and tests.yml

The third runner-inputs bullet is right about what to write and wrong about
why, and it is the one bullet in that list whose mechanism a session would go
and test. [Q35](Q35.md) is the same shape against the first bullet — a
constraint measured elsewhere, filed rather than folded in.

`CLAUDE.md:116:is a step that dies on the common case`, and the comment it was
copied into at the head of the `doctor-mutation-control` step's first `run:`
block in `.github/workflows/tests.yml`, both say an `&&` list whose left side is
false takes the step down where it stands.
It does not. errexit exempts the left side of an `&&` list, so a loop of them
runs to completion at rc=0 — including with the list as the loop body's last
command, which is the shape that comment is attached to.

Measured 2026-08-22 under `bash -e`, on 3.2.57 and 5.3.15, identical on both.
The three shapes that do take the step down:

- the list as the script's last command, whose exit status it becomes
- the list as a called function's last command, where the call is not exempt
  and errexit fires on it
- the command *after* the final `&&` being the one that fails

The remedy is unaffected: `if` is still what to write, and the instrument two
lines below the bullet is correct as it stands. What the wrong reason costs is
the check. A session testing the claim writes the loop, watches it pass at
rc=0, and concludes the bullet is wrong rather than incomplete — so the bullet
that exists to stop one silent failure hands back a second one. A list that
died where it stood would have been caught by the first local drive; the
quietness is the entire reason the bullet earns its place, which is why it has
to name the shapes that are quiet.

Fix both lines, and keep them saying the same thing as each other — the
workflow comment is the copy a reader meets while looking at the code it
justifies.
