# The deadline test's fixture now scans inside its 20 ms budget

`TestTheDeadlineFiresWhileTheMatchLoopIsRunning` gives a 4 MiB fixture a
20 ms budget and expects the deadline to fire mid-scan. Its comment puts the
scan at "about 0.6s of match loop at the worst rate measured for this ruleset,
which is thirty times the budget". The match loop has since sped up, and that
margin is gone.

The test fails intermittently on GitHub's runners. It failed in 2 of the 20
`tests` runs listed on 2026-11-02, on two unrelated branches: run 41400470723
at `b02b29e` (Q219) and run 41403326117 at `610cf60` (Q208). Both failed on the 20 ms arm's `deferred` check: the arm wrote a `deny` on the
planted key rather than deferring, and the whole test took 0.03s. `main` passed in the
same window.

Locally, on 2026-11-01, `go test -run
'^TestTheDeadlineFiresWhileTheMatchLoopIsRunning$' -count=200` passed 200 of
200, at 0.05–0.06s for the whole test, both arms included. So the scan the
comment puts at 0.6s now takes tens of milliseconds, and the margin over the
budget is about 2x rather than 30x. A runner that scans faster than this
machine crosses it.

**`-cpu 1` reproduces it locally, and that sits badly with the paragraph above.**
With `-run TheDeadlineFiresWhileTheMatchLoopIsRunning -count=100`, the worker on
#234 read 0 failures at default parallelism and 77 of 100 with `-cpu 1`, on both
`efac304` and its branch, all at line 158. That PR's reviewer read 74 and 79 on the
same two trees. Less CPU making it fail is the opposite of what "a runner that scans
faster" predicts, and why it fails there is unestablished, so settle that before
rebuilding the ratio.

Q132 says this test does not depend on a fixture size. It does, and this row
is the measurement that says so.

**The fix** rebuilds the ratio against today's rate. Either grow the fixture
until the unhurried arm takes something like 30 times the budget, or shrink
the budget. Then assert that ratio in the test, timing the control arm, so
the next speedup fails loudly instead of flaking. Correct Q132's claim with
it.
