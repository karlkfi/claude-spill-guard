# The clean corpus is 10 files, and two claims rest on more than it holds

`testdata/corpus/clean/` is 10 files and 8,731 bytes, written for the purpose.
It is adversarial — the inherited `pii-postal-code` reports 30 matches on it
and `pii-phone-cn` reports 8 — and it is small. Two things currently lean on it
harder than that supports.

**The pinned false-positive count.** Zero over 8.7 KiB is a much weaker claim
than zero over the 257 files and 27.6% file-level rate the design argues from.
The rules the corpus cannot see are the ones with no case in it: a sample JWT,
a published example key, a base64 blob that happens to open `eyJ` —
[Q60](Q60.md) carries the first two by name. A quoted PEM header was on that
list until the rule grew a body-line requirement, and it was found by scanning
this repository rather than by anyone reading the corpus, which is the argument
for doing that deliberately. Run the ruleset over a real infrastructure
repository, read every hit, and either extend the corpus with what it found or
cut the rule that found it.

**What the clean half is adversarial *against*.** The 30 and 8 floors pin it
against the inherited numeric family, which is the noise this project exists to
answer. It is not adversarial against most of the shipped credential rules: six
of the ten — `github-fine-grained-pat`, `slack-token`, `stripe-live-secret-key`,
`openai-api-key`, `private-key-block`, `jwt` — get zero *raw regex* matches on
the clean half, before any validator runs. For those six the pinned zero is a
zero over material that never approaches them, which is a weaker statement than
it looks. Four rules do get raw matches and are held by their validators; that
is the part currently carrying the claim.

**`validate.DefaultLabelWindow`'s own doc comment.** It records 64 bytes from
37,822 numeric runs over 914 files, then says the corpus was the wrong shape —
no Kubernetes manifests, no Ansible, no logs, no `.env` files — and asks to be
re-taken against `testdata/corpus` once that exists. It exists, and it cannot
answer the question: the measurement needs numeric runs sitting near PII
labels, and a corpus of clean infrastructure files holds none by construction.
So the comment's instruction is not executable as written.

The re-measurement it wants needs a third thing, which is neither half of the
current corpus: real text carrying real labels beside real numbers. Synthesised
PII would answer a question about the synthesiser. Decide whether that corpus
can be assembled at all, and if it cannot, say so in the comment rather than
leaving a re-measurement standing that nobody can take.

Grow the clean half first; the window measurement is downstream of having
material to take it on.
