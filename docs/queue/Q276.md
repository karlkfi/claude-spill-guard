# The walk's floor counts from the walk, not from the hook's start

`walk` in `internal/hook/deadline.go` gives the walk at least `walkFloor`, one second, measured from when the walk starts rather than from `start`, where the budget is measured from.
So a payload that takes more than 59 seconds to arrive on stdin, followed by a walk longer than a second, runs past the hook's 60-second timeout and is killed with no record.
Before Q270, a budget spent by the time the scan was reached deferred at once with a record.

Found by Reviewer B reading #300 at `8dbbf21`; not driven.
Both conditions are needed together, and no harness run has been seen to take the first.

The review proposes capping the floor at `hookTimeout - time.Since(start)`.
Capping it at `start + budget + walkFloor` instead would also keep the margin `TestTheBudgetFitsInsideTheTimeoutTheManifestGives` asserts.
Neither remedy is measured.
A test needs a `start` far enough in the past that the cap binds, and a walk stalled with `stallOn(walkLabel, false)`, and should assert the call defers with a record instead of waiting out the floor.
