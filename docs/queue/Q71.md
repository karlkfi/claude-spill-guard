# Q70's stock reading was invalidated by the commit that filed it

[Q70](Q70.md) argues that `render --label` reports the stock rather than the
flow, and cites the stock to do it: "five rows carry `open-question` today, out
of forty". That was exactly right when it was drafted, and wrong by the time
anyone could read it. Measured 2026-08-27, walking the frontmatter of every
`Q*.md` at each revision:

| Revision | Labelled | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| `139cf76~1` — the tree Q70 was drafted against | 5 — Q15, Q16, Q35, Q44, Q54 | 40 |
| `139cf76` — the commit that filed Q70 | 4 — Q15, Q16, Q35, Q44 | 41 |
| after the branch that re-scoped Q15 | 3 — Q16, Q35, Q44 | 42 |

Both moves in the first step are Q70's own subject arriving on Q70. #38 filed
Q70, which is the row that took the denominator from 40 to 41, and it settled
whether [Q54](Q54.md) is an open question at all, which is the row that took the
numerator from 5 to 4. Neither settled a question in the sense the label tracks,
and the citation was stale before the merge that carried it.

The argument survives. Its point is that a stock cannot separate a class
settling questions from one that has never settled any, and that holds at three
as it did at five. The exhibit does not, and it is doing exhibit work in a row
whose whole subject is that this store reports the wrong quantity.

## The shape, which is worth more than the number

A citation can go stale in three ways and only two of them are watched for. It
can expire, as a pointer does when the line it names moves; its terms can be
redefined under it. This is a third: **the commit carrying the claim changes the
population the claim quantifies over.** Nothing in a diff can show it, because
both halves are correct in isolation — the sentence was true of the tree its
author measured, and the row and the label edit that invalidated it are ordinary
changes sitting in the same commit for good reasons.

It is invisible to the instrument you would reach for first, too. Diffing the
commit shows Q70 added and Q54's frontmatter edited, and neither reads as
touching a number in a third file. What surfaced it was walking every `Q*.md`'s
frontmatter at each revision and counting — an instrument that re-derives the
population instead of tracking the edits to it.

## What to do, and what not to

Do not repair it by writing today's count in — that is the third row of the
table waiting to happen. Either state the count with the revision it was taken
at, the way `docs/design/` dates its measurements, or drop the figure and let
the sentence carry the argument, which it already does. The second is smaller
and cannot go stale.

Re-derive before trusting any of the three:

```
python3 scripts/vendor/claude-skills/queue.py render --label open-question | wc -l
ls docs/queue/Q*.md | wc -l
```
