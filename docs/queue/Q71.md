# The open-question stock in Q70 is a reading of a tree that has moved

[Q70](Q70.md) argues that `render --label` reports the stock rather than the
flow, and cites the stock to do it: "five rows carry `open-question` today, out
of forty". Measured on this tree 2026-08-27, `render --label open-question`
returns **four** — Q15, Q16, Q35 and Q44 — out of 41 rows.

The argument survives the correction. Its point is that a stock cannot separate
a class settling questions from one that has never settled any, and that holds
at four as it did at five. What does not survive is the number, and the number
is doing exhibit work in a row whose whole subject is that this store reports
the wrong quantity.

## Why it will move again before anyone fixes it

The drift is the mechanism Q70 names, arriving on Q70. Both moves it lists
change the stock with no question settled, and a third does settle one: Q15 was
re-scoped from a decision to a measurement on 2026-08-27 and dropped the label,
which is where four became three.

So do not repair this by writing today's count in. Either state the count with
the revision it was taken at, the way the design doc dates its measurements, or
drop the figure and let the sentence carry the argument, which it already does.
The second is smaller and cannot go stale.
